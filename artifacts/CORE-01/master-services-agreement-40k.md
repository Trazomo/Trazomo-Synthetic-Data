# Master Services Agreement

**Between Copperline Software, Inc. and Atticus Dundee Inc.**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Copperline Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 45 Tamarind Loop, Suite 210, Denver, Colorado 80202 ("**Provider**"); and

**Atticus Dundee Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1450 Halverson Quay, Suite 600, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Customer**").

Provider and Customer are each referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in Section 21 (Definitions) or where first defined in the body of this Agreement.

## Recitals

**A.** Provider develops, hosts, and licenses a cloud-based revenue and contract operations platform and provides related implementation, configuration, and support services.

**B.** Customer wishes to subscribe to that platform for use by its personnel and to engage Provider to implement and configure the platform for Customer's environment.

**C.** The Parties wish to establish the general terms that will govern that subscription and those services, and to provide a framework under which Customer may order additional services from time to time by executing Order Forms and Statements of Work that incorporate this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises set out below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Term and Renewal

**1.1 Agreement Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until it is terminated in accordance with Section 7 (Termination) or until the expiration of all Subscription Terms and Statements of Work then in effect, whichever occurs later.

**1.2 Initial Subscription Term.** The initial subscription term for the Subscription Services (the "**Initial Term**") begins on February 1, 2026 (the "**Subscription Start Date**") and continues for twelve (12) consecutive months, ending on January 31, 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

**1.3 Automatic Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, and upon expiration of each Renewal Term thereafter, the Subscription Services will renew automatically for a further period of twelve (12) months (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party delivers to the other Party written notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days before the last day of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. The Initial Term together with each Renewal Term is the "**Subscription Term**."

**1.4 Renewal Notice by Provider.** Provider will deliver to Customer a written renewal notice not less than sixty (60) days before the last day of the then-current Subscription Term. The renewal notice will state (a) the date on which the then-current Subscription Term expires, (b) the Fees that will apply during the next Renewal Term as calculated under Section 5.4, and (c) the date by which Customer must deliver a notice of non-renewal under Section 1.3. If Provider fails to deliver the renewal notice by the date required by this Section 1.4, the deadline for Customer to deliver a notice of non-renewal under Section 1.3 is extended to the date that is fifteen (15) days after Provider's actual delivery of the renewal notice.

**1.5 Late Notice of Non-Renewal.** Any purported notice of non-renewal that is delivered fewer than thirty (30) days before the last day of the then-current Subscription Term is void and of no effect, and the Subscription Services will renew for a further Renewal Term as provided in Section 1.3. A notice of non-renewal delivered during the period beginning ninety (90) days before the last day of the then-current Subscription Term and ending thirty (30) days before that day is effective for the Renewal Term that follows the Renewal Term then in progress, and Customer remains responsible for the Fees for the Renewal Term that has commenced.

**1.6 Effect on Statements of Work.** Expiration or non-renewal of a Subscription Term does not by itself terminate any Statement of Work then in progress. Each Statement of Work continues in accordance with its own terms and this Agreement continues to govern it until it is completed or terminated.

## 2. Subscription Services and Professional Services

**2.1 Subscription Grant.** Subject to Customer's compliance with this Agreement and payment of the applicable Fees, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right during the Subscription Term to access and use the Platform and the Documentation for Customer's internal business purposes, up to the number of Authorized Users and within the usage limits set out in Exhibit A.

**2.2 Affiliates.** Customer's Affiliates may access and use the Platform under Customer's subscription. Customer is responsible for each Affiliate's compliance with this Agreement, and any act or omission of an Affiliate that would be a breach of this Agreement if committed by Customer is a breach by Customer.

**2.3 Professional Services.** Provider will perform implementation, configuration, data migration, integration, and training services (collectively, "**Professional Services**") as described in Exhibit B and in any Statement of Work executed by the Parties. Each Statement of Work must identify the services, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, assumptions, fees, and Provider personnel roles, and must reference this Agreement by its title and Effective Date.

**2.4 Support and Service Levels.** Provider will provide support and will make the Platform available in accordance with the service levels set out in Exhibit D. Service Credits issued under Exhibit D are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for Provider's failure to meet the availability commitment set out in Exhibit D, except where Customer terminates for chronic failure under Section 7.1(c).

**2.5 Changes to the Platform.** Provider may modify the Platform from time to time, provided that Provider will not materially decrease the core functionality of the Platform, materially degrade the security controls described in Exhibit C, or materially reduce the availability commitment set out in Exhibit D during any Subscription Term. Provider will give Customer at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice of any deprecation of a feature that Customer is then using in production.

**2.6 Change Orders.** Any change to the scope, schedule, deliverables, or fees of a Statement of Work must be documented in a written change order signed by an authorized representative of each Party. Provider is not obligated to perform, and Customer is not obligated to pay for, work outside an executed Statement of Work or change order.

## 3. Customer Responsibilities and Restrictions

**3.1 Customer Responsibilities.** Customer will (a) provide Provider with the access, information, personnel, environments, and decisions reasonably necessary for Provider to perform, in accordance with the assumptions and Customer dependencies stated in Exhibit B; (b) designate a project sponsor and a day-to-day project manager with authority to make decisions and issue Acceptance Certificates; (c) be responsible for the accuracy and legality of Customer Data; and (d) maintain the network, hardware, and third-party software required to access the Platform.

**3.2 Authorized Users.** Customer will ensure that each Authorized User keeps login credentials confidential and does not share them. Customer will promptly notify Provider of any known or suspected unauthorized use of Customer's account. Customer may reassign a subscription seat from one individual to another, but may not permit two or more individuals to use a single seat concurrently.

**3.3 Restrictions.** Customer will not, and will not permit any Authorized User or third party to: (a) sell, resell, license, sublicense, rent, or lease the Platform, or make the Platform available to any third party other than Authorized Users; (b) reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Platform, or attempt to derive its source code, except to the extent that restriction is prohibited by Applicable Law; (c) copy, modify, or create derivative works of the Platform or the Documentation; (d) use the Platform to build a competing product or service, or to benchmark the Platform for publication without Provider's prior written consent; (e) introduce malicious code into the Platform or interfere with its integrity or performance; or (f) use the Platform in violation of Applicable Law.

**3.4 Customer Dependency Delays.** If Customer fails to meet a Customer dependency identified in Exhibit B or a Statement of Work, and that failure delays Provider's performance, the affected milestone dates will be extended day for day, and Provider may invoice Customer for reasonable, documented standby costs actually incurred, provided that Provider (i) notifies Customer in writing within five (5) Business Days of becoming aware of the delay, and (ii) takes commercially reasonable steps to redeploy affected personnel.

## 4. Implementation, Milestones, and Acceptance

**4.1 Implementation Plan.** Provider will perform the implementation described in Exhibit B (Implementation Plan and Milestones) in four phases, each concluding in a milestone (each, a "**Milestone**"). Exhibit B sets out the deliverables, acceptance criteria, target dates, and Customer dependencies for each Milestone.

**4.2 Acceptance Procedure.** Provider will notify Customer in writing when it believes a Milestone deliverable is complete. Customer will have ten (10) Business Days from that notice to evaluate the deliverable against the acceptance criteria stated in Exhibit B and to either (a) issue a written acceptance certificate (an "**Acceptance Certificate**") or (b) deliver a written notice of rejection identifying with reasonable particularity each failure to meet the stated acceptance criteria. If Customer does not deliver an Acceptance Certificate or a notice of rejection within that ten (10) Business Day period, the deliverable is deemed accepted.

**4.3 Correction and Re-Submission.** If Customer rejects a deliverable, Provider will correct the identified failures and re-submit the deliverable within fifteen (15) Business Days, and the procedure in Section 4.2 will apply again to the re-submitted deliverable. If Provider fails to achieve acceptance of the same deliverable after two (2) re-submissions, Customer may, at its option, (a) accept the deliverable subject to an equitable reduction in the associated Milestone payment, or (b) terminate the affected Statement of Work for cause under Section 7.1(a) without any further cure period and receive a refund of amounts paid for the rejected deliverable.

**4.4 Milestone Gate.** Provider will not commence any Phase 3 (Production Configuration and Cutover Readiness) activity, and Customer will not be obligated to pay for any Phase 3 activity, until Customer has issued an Acceptance Certificate for Milestone 2 (Integration Readiness) under Section 4.2. Any Phase 3 work performed before that Acceptance Certificate is issued is performed at Provider's own risk and expense. If Milestone 2 has not been accepted within thirty (30) days after the target date stated in Exhibit B, the Parties' project sponsors will meet within five (5) Business Days to agree a written recovery plan, and the remaining Milestone target dates will be re-baselined by change order under Section 2.6.

**4.5 No Waiver by Acceptance.** Acceptance of a deliverable does not waive any warranty in Section 13 or any right or remedy in respect of a defect that was not reasonably discoverable during the acceptance period.

## 5. Fees, Invoicing, and Payment

**5.1 Fees.** Customer will pay the fees set out in Exhibit A (the "**Fees**"). Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, all Fees are non-refundable and all payment obligations are non-cancellable.

**5.2 Subscription Fees.** The Subscription Fee for the Initial Term is **forty thousand dollars ($40,000)**, invoiced in advance on the Subscription Start Date and covering the period from February 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027. Subscription Fees are payable in advance for each Subscription Term and are earned ratably across the applicable Subscription Term.

**5.3 Professional Services Fees.** Professional Services described in Exhibit B are provided on a fixed-fee basis of **seven thousand five hundred dollars ($7,500)** for the Initial Term implementation, invoiced against Milestones as set out in Exhibit A. Professional Services ordered under any additional Statement of Work will be invoiced as that Statement of Work provides.

**5.4 Annual Uplift.** The Subscription Fee payable for each Renewal Term is equal to one hundred seven percent (107%) of the Subscription Fee payable for the immediately preceding Subscription Term, representing an annual increase of seven percent (7%). This uplift applies automatically and does not require notice other than the renewal notice described in Section 1.4. Any additional increase attributable to added Authorized Users, added modules, or usage above the limits stated in Exhibit A is calculated separately and in addition to the uplift under this Section 5.4.

**5.5 Invoicing and Payment Terms.** Provider will invoice Customer as provided in Exhibit A. Customer will pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date, in U.S. dollars, by electronic funds transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing. Provider will not change its remittance instructions except by written notice signed by an officer of Provider and confirmed by Customer by telephone to a previously known contact.

**5.6 Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value-added, and similar transaction taxes. Customer is responsible for those taxes other than taxes based on Provider's net income, property, or employees. Customer will provide a valid exemption certificate where applicable.

**5.7 Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold payment of any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith, provided that Customer (a) pays all undisputed amounts when due and (b) notifies Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute within twenty (20) days after the invoice date. The Parties will work in good faith to resolve any disputed amount within thirty (30) days after that notice. Amounts properly disputed under this Section 5.7 are not overdue and do not accrue interest under Section 5.8 while the dispute is pending.

**5.8 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law, calculated from the due date until paid.

**5.9 Suspension for Non-Payment.** If any undisputed amount remains unpaid more than forty-five (45) days after its due date, Provider may suspend the Subscription Services after giving Customer at least ten (10) Business Days' prior written notice and an opportunity to cure. Provider will restore access promptly after payment. Suspension under this Section 5.9 does not relieve Customer of its payment obligations and does not extend the Subscription Term.

**5.10 Expenses.** Provider will invoice reasonable and documented travel and lodging expenses incurred at Customer's request only, subject to Customer's written pre-approval and to Customer's then-current travel and expense policy provided to Provider. Expenses invoiced under this Section 5.10 will not exceed five percent (5%) of the Professional Services fees stated in Section 5.3 without Customer's prior written approval.

## 7. Termination

**7.1 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement, any Order Form, or any Statement of Work, in whole or in part, by written notice to the other Party if:

(a) the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure that breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail;

(b) the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, files or has filed against it a petition in bankruptcy that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or has a receiver appointed over a material part of its assets; or

(c) in the case of Customer, the Platform fails to meet the monthly availability commitment set out in Exhibit D in any three (3) months within a rolling six (6) month period, in which case Customer may terminate on thirty (30) days' written notice given within forty-five (45) days after the third such month.

**7.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any Statement of Work, for any reason or no reason upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party. If Provider terminates under this Section 7.2, Provider will refund to Customer a pro-rata portion of any prepaid Subscription Fees corresponding to the remainder of the then-current Subscription Term, calculated from the effective date of termination. If Customer terminates under this Section 7.2, prepaid Subscription Fees are not refundable, and Customer will pay for all Professional Services performed and non-cancellable commitments incurred through the effective date of termination.

**7.3 Effect of Termination.** On the effective date of expiration or termination: (a) all rights granted to Customer under Section 2.1 cease; (b) Customer will pay all amounts accrued and unpaid as of that date; (c) each Party will comply with Section 10.5 in respect of the other Party's Confidential Information; and (d) Provider will make Customer Data available for export as provided in Section 9.6.

**7.4 Transition Assistance.** On Customer's written request made no later than thirty (30) days after the effective date of expiration or termination, Provider will provide reasonable transition assistance for a period of up to ninety (90) days, at Provider's then-current time-and-materials rates, to support the migration of Customer Data and configuration to Customer or to a successor provider. Provider will provide transition assistance under this Section 7.4 regardless of the reason for termination, provided that Customer pays undisputed amounts then due.

**7.5 Survival.** Sections 3.3, 5 (as to amounts accrued before termination), 6, 7.3, 7.4, 7.5, 8, 9.6, 10, 12, 13.4, 16, 19, 20, and 21 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 9. Data Protection and Security

**9.1 Data Processing Addendum.** The Parties' respective obligations in relation to Personal Data are set out in the Data Processing Addendum attached as **Exhibit C** (the "**DPA**"), which is incorporated into and forms part of this Agreement. In the event of a conflict between the DPA and the body of this Agreement in respect of the processing of Personal Data, the DPA controls.

**9.2 Roles.** As between the Parties and in respect of Customer Data, Customer is the controller (or business) and Provider is the processor (or service provider). Provider will process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, which include this Agreement, the DPA, and Customer's use of the Platform, unless Applicable Law requires otherwise, in which case Provider will notify Customer before processing unless that notice is prohibited by law.

**9.3 Security Program.** Provider will maintain an information security program that meets or exceeds the administrative, physical, and technical safeguards described in Schedule C-2 to the DPA, including encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest, role-based access control, logging, vulnerability management, and annual penetration testing. Provider will maintain a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Platform and will provide the most recent report to Customer on request, no more than once per calendar year, subject to Section 10.

**9.4 Security Incidents.** Provider will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Data, and will provide the information reasonably necessary for Customer to meet its own notification obligations. Provider will investigate each Security Incident, take reasonable steps to mitigate its effects, and provide Customer with a written incident report within fifteen (15) Business Days after containment.

**9.5 Subprocessors.** Provider may engage the subprocessors listed in **Exhibit E**. Provider will give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before adding or replacing a subprocessor, and Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection, Customer may terminate the affected Subscription Services under Section 7.1(a) without further cure period and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid Subscription Fees. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subprocessors as if they were Provider's own.

**9.6 Return and Deletion.** During the Subscription Term and for thirty (30) days after its expiration or termination, Provider will make Customer Data available for export in a structured, machine-readable format at no additional charge. After that period, Provider will delete Customer Data within a further sixty (60) days, except for copies retained in routine backups (which Provider will delete on its ordinary backup cycle) or as required by Applicable Law, and will certify deletion in writing on Customer's request.

**9.7 No Training on Customer Data.** Provider will not use Customer Data to train, fine-tune, or otherwise develop any machine-learning model made available to any party other than Customer. Provider may use aggregated and de-identified usage statistics that do not identify Customer, any Authorized User, or any individual to operate and improve the Platform.
