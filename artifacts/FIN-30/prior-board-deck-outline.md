# Board Deck Outline: Q4 2025 Quarterly Review

**Entity:** Atticus Dundee Inc.
**Document type:** the standing outline the quarterly board deck is built from
**Owner:** VP, Finance
**Maintained by:** Controller

This is the standing Q4 2025 board agenda as an outline and nothing else. It
carries the section headings in the order the meeting takes them, the figure
slots named and left empty, the distribution list, and the classification
convention the pack already uses. It carries no narrative and no figure of its
own. Building a deck from it is separate work from maintaining it.

## Classification and handling

An outline is unclassified until it is populated. The moment a figure slot is
filled, the populated deck inherits the classification the board material
convention assigns, and the banner that convention specifies is applied at the
head and the foot of the populated file.

This outline does not duplicate the board pack excerpt, which stays the
classification and handling document: the outline points at the classification
convention rather than restating the banner. When the convention changes there
is one document to change, and it is not this one.

## How the figure slots are filled

The figure slots are named and empty on purpose, so every number a narrative
states has to come from the approved metrics pack with the source reference
that pack carries for it.

1. A slot is filled from the approved metrics pack and from nothing else. Not
   from a working file, not from a draft schedule, not from a recollection of
   the last review.
2. A filled slot travels with the source reference the pack gives the metric,
   so a reader can go from the slide back to the record behind it.
3. A slot with no approved metric stays empty and is presented as empty. An
   empty slot is a status the board can act on. An unsourced number is a
   finding.
4. A metric the pack marks as draft keeps that label inside the slot, and the
   label travels onto the slide with it.
5. A slot that the pack cannot fill is raised with the owner named beside the
   section below, before the deck is circulated, never in the meeting.

## Distribution

Distribution is by role title. No individual is named in this outline, and no
signature line is required, because an outline is not approved by signing it.

| Recipient | Standing in the meeting |
|---|---|
| Board of Directors | Receives the deck and holds the executive session |
| Chief Executive Officer | Chairs the meeting and owns the opening, review, strategic and decision sections |
| VP, Finance | Owns the financial results, key metrics, and risk and compliance sections |
| Director, Finance | Owns the budget and forecast section |
| Controller | Owns the close and controls section and presents the financial results |
| FP&A Analyst | Presents the key metrics and the budget and forecast sections |

The distribution list and every owner above are role titles from the employee
ladder, which is what lets the outline outlive any one holder of a seat. A
person name belongs on the attendance record, not in this document.

## What this outline does not carry

No money amount and no percentage appears anywhere in this document, and no
date inside the current reporting period. That is on purpose. A number typed
into an outline outlives the quarter it was true for, and the outline is reused
every quarter. The figures live in the approved metrics pack, the handling
rules live in the board pack excerpt, and this file stays the structure the
meeting follows.
