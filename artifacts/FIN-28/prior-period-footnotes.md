**How this set is read.** Each footnote declares in its own heading the close
checklist category whose balance it reports. That is how a reader gets from a
disclosure back to the close work that supports it, and it names the support
the way an audit ready footnote is expected to. It is a category rather than an
account code because not every close task carries an account code, and a
footnote is supported by the work rather than by the ledger line. A footnote
that reports no close checklist balance declares no category and says so in its
heading.

**What is stated here and what is not.** Every figure below is the February
amount recorded in the account named beside it, to the cent, as the ledger
states it. Balances carried on the statement of financial position are
described here and reported there. This set travels with the February
statements and does not restate them, because a balance stated in two places
is a reconciliation nobody asked for.

## 1. Revenue recognition and deferred revenue (close checklist category: revenue)

Revenue from subscription contracts is recognized ratably over the contract
term as the service is made available. Revenue from professional services is
recognized as the service is performed. Amounts invoiced ahead of performance
are recorded as deferred revenue, current and non-current, and released to
revenue as the related performance obligation is satisfied.

## 2. Accrued liabilities (close checklist category: accruals)

Accrued liabilities are obligations for goods and services received, and for
employee compensation earned, that are unbilled or unpaid at period end. They
are recorded in four accounts: Accrued Liabilities (2010), Accrued Payroll
(2020), Accrued Bonus (2030) and Accrued Commissions (2040). Each accrual is
measured against the charge recorded in the accounts it supports, and is
relieved as the underlying amount is invoiced or paid.

The payroll, bonus and commission accruals (2020, 2030 and 2040) are measured
against the compensation accounts they support. The expense recognized in
those compensation accounts in February 2026 was:

| Account | Description | February 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| 6000 | Salaries and Wages | $2,290,002.83 |
| 6010 | Payroll Taxes | $253,790.71 |
| 6020 | Employee Benefits | $283,065.12 |
| 6030 | Contractors and Consultants | $125,483.97 |

Accrued Liabilities (2010) is the accrual for goods and services received and
not yet invoiced, and it is not in the table above. It is measured from the
receipt records rather than from a single expense account, because the charge
it relieves lands in whichever account consumed the goods or the service.

The accrued balances in accounts 2010, 2020, 2030 and 2040 at 2026-02-28 are
carried on the statement of financial position. No accrual in this footnote
rests on an estimate that a later period is expected to revise materially.

## 3. Prepaid expenses and amortization (close checklist category: accruals)

Prepaid expenses are amounts paid ahead of the period they benefit. They are
recorded as an asset when paid and amortized to expense over the period
benefited, on a schedule kept per prepayment and updated each period as part of
the close.

The expense recognized in February 2026 in the accounts the prepaid schedules
amortize into was:

| Account | Description | February 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| 6600 | Insurance | $49,199.81 |
| 6200 | Software Subscriptions | $639,150.86 |

Depreciation and amortization of long lived assets is a separate charge and is
not part of the prepaid schedules. It was $345,165.91 for the period, in account
6800. The prepaid balances at 2026-02-28 are carried on the statement of
financial position.

This footnote reports no close checklist balance and declares no category. It
