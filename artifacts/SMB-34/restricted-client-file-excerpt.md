## 4. Financing terms

| item_id | item | value |
|---|---|---|
| RCF-LDB-04 | amount financed, the second and third draws | $89,100.00 |
| RCF-LDB-05 | term | 180 months |
| RCF-LDB-06 | rate | a fixed 6.90 percent |

The homeowners pay the second and third draws from a renovation loan in their own names, at a fixed rate over the term above, and they repay their lender monthly themselves. The deposit and the final draw come from their own funds. The contract sum is $148,500.00 with a 20 percent deposit, as in the approved proposal, and the studio's own terms are unchanged by the financing: every draw is invoiced to the homeowners and paid by them. No lender, no account and no loan product is recorded here.

The studio holds these terms only so that the project lead can answer a payment question without asking the homeowners to repeat them. They are never restated to anyone, including the homeowners, in writing.
