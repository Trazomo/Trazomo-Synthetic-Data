## Scope of work

The kitchen is a full replacement. The existing kitchen comes out to the studs where the layout changes and to the finish surface everywhere else, and what goes back is new throughout: cabinetry along the run and the return, quartz countertops fabricated to the finished cabinet line, tile to the floor and to the wall behind the counter, and paint once the finish carpentry is closed up. Plumbing is rough in and fixture set at the sink position, and electrical is rough in and device set for the outlets, the switching and the lighting the new layout needs.

The primary bath is a renovation on the existing footprint. Demolition takes the room back to a working substrate, the plumbing rough in and fixture set follows the same trades that carry the kitchen, and the fixture and vanity package is supplied and installed as one lot. Tile runs to the floor and to the wet wall, finish carpentry sets the vanity and the trim, and paint closes the room out. The footprint does not move.

Both rooms sit under one set of construction documents and one supervision line. Design development and construction documents are produced first and are the basis every trade works to. Project management and supervision runs from demolition to the final finishes and covers trade sequencing, site protection and disposal. A contingency allowance is carried in the bill for the concealed conditions demolition finds. Work outside the kitchen and the primary bath is not in this proposal.

## Allowances and exclusions

The contingency allowance is carried as line `PLI-LDB-12` at $4,500.00 and it is an allowance rather than a fee. It is drawn against only for concealed conditions that demolition uncovers, it is drawn in writing with the condition described, and any part not drawn by substantial completion is credited back at closeout.

The cabinetry line is priced per linear foot at the run shown in the construction documents, and the tile line is priced per square foot at the quantity those documents take off. A selection the homeowners make at a higher supply rate than the appendix carries, or a quantity above the take off, is a change to the contract sum rather than a draw against the contingency allowance.

Excluded from this proposal: work outside the kitchen and the primary bath; structural alteration of any kind; appliances, which the homeowners supply for the studio to set and connect; furnishings and window treatments; and any condition that requires a design change beyond the contingency allowance, which is quoted separately before it starts.

## Payment schedule

Terms are `net_7` on each draw, so each draw is due within seven days of the date it is issued. The schedule is four draws stated as percentages of the contract sum, with the figures following.

- Deposit, issued on contract signing: 20 percent of the contract sum, $29,700.00.
- Second draw, issued at demolition complete: 30 percent of the contract sum, $44,550.00.
- Third draw, issued at rough in complete: 30 percent of the contract sum, $44,550.00.
- Final draw, issued at substantial completion: 20 percent of the contract sum, $29,700.00.

The four draws sum to the contract sum of $148,500.00. No draw is issued ahead of the stage it is tied to, and the final draw is issued at substantial completion rather than at closeout, so the closeout walk through is never held against a balance. Payments answer these four draws and nothing else: no other charge, fee or rate uplift appears anywhere in this proposal.
